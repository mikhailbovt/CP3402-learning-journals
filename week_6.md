# CP3402 Learning Journal - Mikhail Bovt

## Week 6

## Learning Activities & Resources

In the past week, my attention was devoted to creating the cooperative environment and starting work on our collaborative project. I made sure to meet my teammates on LinkedIn, subscribe to the group GitHub repository to help in the organisation of the GitHub Project board. 

We chose Discord as the core communication medium because of the fact that it allows both text and voice interaction, screen sharing, individual topic channels, and integration of bots at one place. Discord was used in order to conduct our first meeting, explain our project idea and maintain communication at all levels.

We chose the second-hand marketplace as the main idea of our project in the course of the meeting and discussed such crucial features that we would like to incorporate in our platform. They include, but are not limited to user accounts, product listing, categories, searching, saving, and communication between buyers and sellers. We also put forth the first distribution of responsibilities among the members of the project team and the initial set of job assignments to complete.
 
I also utilized the bot I had created earlier to summarize the meeting in Discord. The bot processed the audio of the meeting and created a comprehensive summary of the meeting with main decisions, tasks for action, assigned responsibilities, unresolved questions, and possible obstacles.
## Estimated Hours of Explicit Learning Activity

4 hours

## Content Insights

The major takeaway from this week was that collaboration tools prove to be effective only when they adequately address demands of a project team. Discord fit into our workflow because the service allowed engaging in ongoing text communication, making voice calls, showing the screen, and automating tasks all in a single platform. GitHub and GitHub Projects complemented Discord as they provided us with a framework for version control, issue monitoring, and task management. 

It has also come to my knowledge that meeting summaries can facilitate the work on projects. Here we did not need to rely on memory or take notes; the Discord bot turned an informal conversation into the decisions made and actions agreed upon. However, that written output still needed to be double-checked since transcription and interpretation errors could have affected the quality of the text.

## Career/Employability/Learning Insights

The week's events demonstrate how a real software development project is initialized. The preparation of communication channels, repository setup, project board management, responsibilities allocation, and decisions documentation entails moderate technical prowess and managerial capability.

The creation and testing of a Discord-summary bot brings out the utility of AI in the process of team working. The bot served to take care of monotonous bureaucratic tasks and turned a real meeting into an effective record of a project. Given my interest in AI engineering and backend development, the current case serves to develop technology-related skills like automation, application programming interface, data management, and tool development according to a real life coordination problem.

I also got a more profound insight into an assessment of the tools based on their utility instead of commonality - using Discord as a communication tool and GitHub Projects as a planning tool resulted in the system, which was easy to use and understand for our team.
